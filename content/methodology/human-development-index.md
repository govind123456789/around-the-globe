## About the index

The Human Development Index (HDI) is published by the United Nations Development Programme's Human Development Report Office. It summarises three dimensions of human development: a long and healthy life, knowledge, and a decent standard of living.

**This site shows UNDP's values and ranks exactly as published** in the Human Development Report 2025, which covers data for 2023. We don't change them. We recompute the index from its components only to check that our copy of the data is complete and correct.

## Comparing over time

UNDP revises earlier years whenever it publishes a new report, so values from different reports should not be compared. All the historical values on this site come from the same HDR 2025 time series. The rank change shown compares each country's 2023 rank with where its 2022 value from the same series would have ranked.
