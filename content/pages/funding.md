## Who pays

At launch the founder pays the running costs. Because the site is static, hosting costs very little; the main variable cost will be the AI assistant, which will run under a hard daily spending cap.

## How we plan to stay independent

- **Grants first.** The plan is to seek grants from funders of open knowledge and open data. Recipients and amounts will be listed here.
- **No single funder above 60%.** No one source will provide more than 60% of income once there is more than one.
- **Donations** will be welcome but will never buy influence over rankings.
- **Any paid services** (for example commissioned indices or a service-level API for institutions) will be disclosed here, and kept separate from the public rankings under the [editorial policy](/about/editorial-policy/).
