## Short version

There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

## What is collected

- **Analytics.** Privacy-friendly, cookieless page-view counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to see which pages are useful. These do not identify you.
- **Your settings.** Your light/dark theme choice is stored in your own browser. Index Builder and Compare settings live in the page address and are never sent to a server.

## The AI assistant (when it launches)

Questions will be processed to produce an answer and cached in anonymised form so that repeat questions are cheaper to answer. They will not be sent to any AI provider's free tier that uses your data for training. If you use your own API key, it stays in your browser.
