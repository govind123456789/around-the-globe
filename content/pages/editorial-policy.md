## Independence

Rankings and methodologies are decided on their merits. No country, organisation or funder can pay to change a score, a weight, a ranking position or the wording of an analysis. If the platform ever sells services (for example a commissioned index), the work will be clearly labelled and disclosed, and kept separate from the public rankings.

## Conflicts of interest

Funders and their relationship to any ranked entity are listed on the [funding page](/about/funding/). Anyone who works on a ranking declares any interest in the entities it covers.

## Original indices

- Methodology, weights and limitations are published before or alongside every version.
- A version is never edited after publication. Changes create a new version with a change-log entry.
- Sensitivity to the weights is tested and shown.

## Data stories and AI

Data stories are written or reviewed by a person before publication. AI-generated text is labelled, uses only figures from the published release, and links each figure to its source value.

## Sensitive topics

Military, geopolitical and political rankings carry a high risk of misuse. The platform does not currently publish them. If it ever does, it will not imply that a single number can predict the outcome of a conflict.
