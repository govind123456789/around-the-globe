Calculation

  1. Eligibility. Countries with a population of at least {{ '{:,}'.format(r.definition.eligibility.min_population) }}.
  2. Data window. For each indicator, the latest value from {{ r.definition.reference_year - r.definition.max_data_age }} to {{ r.definition.reference_year }}.
  3. Normalisation. Min–max across eligible countries, so 1 = best and 0 = worst. Indicators marked “log” are log-transformed first, and lower-is-better indicators are flipped.
  4. Pillar score. The weighted mean of the indicators a country has, re-weighted over the indicators with data.
  5. Index score. The weighted mean of the pillar scores × 100.
  6. Coverage rule. A country is ranked only if the indicators it has data for carry at least {{ (r.definition.min_coverage * 100)|int }}% of the total weight. Missing values are never treated as zero.
  7. Ties. Equal scores (to 6 decimal places) share a rank, shown as “=”.
  8. Sensitivity. {{ '{:,}'.format(r.definition.mc.runs) }} runs, each multiplying every pillar and indicator weight by a random factor between {{ 1 - r.definition.mc.spread }} and {{ 1 + r.definition.mc.spread }} (seed {{ r.definition.mc.seed }}). The published rank range is the 5th–95th percentile of each country’s rank. {{ r.sensitivity_note }}
