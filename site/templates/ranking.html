How it’s calculated

{% if composite %}

Each indicator is rescaled 0–1 across eligible countries (min–max; log first for {{ pillars|map(attribute='indicators')|sum(start=[])|selectattr('transform', 'equalto', 'log')|map(attribute='id')|map('ename_ind')|join(', ') }}). Lower-is-better indicators are flipped. Indicators are averaged into pillars, and pillars into a 0–100 score:
