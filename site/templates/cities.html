How “city” is defined. These are the European Commission’s urban centres: continuous areas of dense, built-up land with at least 50,000 people, drawn the same way in every country. They ignore municipal borders, so one urban centre can hold several administrative cities (Guangzhou’s includes much of the Pearl River Delta), and its population can differ a lot from official city figures.
