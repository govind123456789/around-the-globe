What this page does not show

Ammunition stocks, counts of tanks, aircraft or ships by type, and classified or “special” projects are not published as open data. Governments rarely disclose them, and the databases that estimate them (IISS, SIPRI, Janes) do not allow reuse here. The equipment section above is the Factbook’s own summary of what each military operates and where it buys it.
