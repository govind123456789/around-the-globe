Time twins

Which country, and which year, looked most like each country does today? Twins are matched on five measures of development: income per person, life expectancy, children per woman, the urban share and child mortality.

{{ lm.lab_nav('/lab/twins/') }}

How it works: each measure is standardised across all countries and years since 1990 (income and child mortality on a log scale), and the closest country-year is found by distance across all five, looking at least eight years back. A small distance means a close match; it says nothing about culture, politics or the path that led there.
