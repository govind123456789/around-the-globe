← {{ c.name }} todayJSON
Modern countries did not exist for most of this period, so this page follows the land inside {{ c.name }}’s present borders (India’s official depiction). Historical borders are approximate and come from Cliopatria, which records “one version” of each polity’s territory; historians disagree on many of them. Shares are of today’s territory. The maps show who held or controlled the land in practice, including military occupation; they are not a judgement on legal sovereignty or on any present-day claim.
