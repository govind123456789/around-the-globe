About this data

Open sources only

Figures come from the World Bank’s World Development Indicators, Ember’s electricity data, the OECD and UNDP, all under licences that allow reuse with credit. Proprietary market-research estimates are not used.

Shares of the world

A country’s share of a market divides its value by the source’s own world total for the same year. Money values are in current US dollars, so exchange rates move them as well as real growth.

Download everything

Every market page has a CSV of the latest value and year of each indicator for every country. Full histories are in the bulk downloads and the API.
