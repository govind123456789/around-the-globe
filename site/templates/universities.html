Universities by research citations

The {{ unis|length|fmt(0) }} universities whose research has been cited most, from the open OpenAlex catalogue of scholarly works. Sort by any column, or pick a country.

What this measures. Citations and publications count research volume and reach, which favours large, old, research-heavy and English-publishing institutions. They say nothing about teaching, cost or student experience. OpenAlex links papers to institutions automatically, so some counts include errors, and university systems or medical schools may be counted separately. The h-index is the largest number h such that h works have at least h citations each. “2-year citedness” is the average citations in the last year of works published in the two years before, which favours recent impact over size.
