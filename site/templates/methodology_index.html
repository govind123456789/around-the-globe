Methodology

How every number on this site is produced, and the rules it follows.

Three kinds of output

KindWhat it isHow you can tell
ObservedA value published by a sourceA source chip with the data year, e.g. World Bank · 2023
CalculatedA score or rank produced by a published formulaOriginal index or Official badge, linked to its methodology version
InterpretationAnalysis written by an editor or generated by AIA separate block, labelled AI where applicable, citing its figures

Rules

  • Missing values are never replaced with zero. A country without enough data is shown as “not ranked” with the reason.
  • Each value shows the year it describes, not the date it was downloaded.
  • Modelled estimates (for example ILO unemployment) are marked EST.
  • Official indices are reproduced with their owner’s own method and labelled as theirs.
  • Original indices are labelled as the platform’s own. Their weights are published and tested for sensitivity.
  • Every published ranking version is immutable. Changing weights creates a new version.
  • Only data whose licence allows reuse and changes is published. This is enforced automatically on every build.
  • Absolute size (GDP, population) and per-person measures are kept separate.
