Reading the numbers

Pearson r

How closely the dots follow a straight line, from −1 to +1. With a log scale ticked, it is calculated on the logged values, which is usually right for incomes and totals.

Spearman ρ

The same idea using ranks instead of values, so a few extreme countries can’t dominate it and curved relationships still count. It doesn’t change with log scales.

R² and the line

R² is the share of the spread in y that the straight line accounts for. A high R² describes a pattern; it does not show that x causes y.
