Size measures are taken on a log scale (each tenfold increase counts the same), then scaled 0–100 across the {{ power|length }} countries of a million people or more. Diplomatic reach counts a permanent Security Council seat as half the measure and membership of the G7, G20, NATO, BRICS, SCO, OECD, EU, Quad and AUKUS (weighted by importance) as the other half.
